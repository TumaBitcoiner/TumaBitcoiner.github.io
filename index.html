Welcome!

Hi! Tuma Here. I'm an engineer and open-source specialist, writing about open-source development.

I follow the Bitcoin and Freedom Tech ecosystem daily, writing about the latest developments and technical news.

I currently work for the following companies in the Bitcoin ecosystem:

  • Open-source Reporter @ Bitcoin++ Insider Edition
  • Contributor @ Bitcoin Optech
  • Technical Editor and Writer @ OpenSats

I also launched my personal newsletter, completely in Italian, called Il Caffè Cypherpunk. This publication has the goal to provide the latest news from the Bitcoin and Freedom Tech worlds to the Italian ecosystem in an accessible way, so that even non-techy people can stay up-to-date.

If you are looking for someone to write technical article about your project, research specific topics for you, or you need editing work feel free to contact me!
